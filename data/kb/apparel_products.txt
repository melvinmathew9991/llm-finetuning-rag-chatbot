Printable Design for Apparel Products

Available in: AU, CA, EU, NZ, US with minimum quantity per order of 1 piece and maximum of 50 pieces. Printing goes direct to garment printing. Adult T Shirts for Men and Women are available in sizes S, M, L, XL, XXL in white, black, navy, light steel, deep royal, deep red, smoke gray and ash colors. Kids T Shirts are available in Youth (S, M and L) in colors white, black, navy, gray. Royal Blue, Red and Charcoal Kids T Shirts are only available in CA and US. Toddlers T shirts are available in sizes 2, 3 and 4 and white, black, navy, gray, light blue, pink colors. Charcoal Toddler T-Shirt is only available in CA and US. Infant onesies are available in 4 different sizes (New Born, 6 Months, 12 months and 18 Months) in White, Black, NAvy, Gray, Light Blue, Pink colors. Sweatshirts are available in sizes S, M, L, XL, XXL and Black, Navy, Heather Charcoal (only AU, CA, NZ, US), Heather Gray (AU and NZ only) and Gray (EU Only). All apparel is printed to order with a standard production time of 3-5 business days before shipping; rush production is available at checkout for an additional fee on most items. Garments should be machine washed cold, inside out, and tumble dried on low - do not iron directly over a printed design and avoid bleach, as high heat and harsh chemicals can crack or fade direct-to-garment prints over time. Unworn, unprinted stock items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund; custom-printed apparel is final sale unless the print itself arrives defective or doesn't match the approved proof.
